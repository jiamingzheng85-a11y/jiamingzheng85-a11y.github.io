Yunhu Qingxin: A Cloud-Edge Collaborative System for Student Mental Health Assessment and Intelligent Intervention

Second contributor, Huadi Cup project, Aug 2024 - Aug 2025. This project addressed mental health assessment through a multimodal intelligent intervention system, with particular emphasis on privacy-preserving deployment on edge devices.

Optimal Timing Selection for NIPT Based on Survival Functions

Principal investigator, Gaojiaoshe Cup project, Sep 2025. I modeled the data using GAM and Kaplan-Meier survival curves, applied SMOTE for oversampling, and trained a random forest classifier. The final model achieved an accuracy of 0.88 and a recall of 0.53 on the test set.

Second lead, University Robotics Competition project, Oct 2025. I was responsible for LiDAR-based map modeling, waypoint selection, baseline algorithm development, and parameter tuning. I also incorporated deep-learning-based arrow recognition and boundary-line modeling to improve driving stability.
